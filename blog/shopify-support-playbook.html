How Shopify Stores Use AI Chatbots to Cut Customer Support Tickets by 70%

By Wilson Guchu • November 12, 2026 • 4 min read

In modern e-commerce, over 70% of inbound customer questions boil down to the same repetitive topics: "Where is my package?", "What is your return policy?", "How does this fit compared to standard sizing?", and "Do you ship internationally?".

The Late-Night Cart Abandonment Problem

A huge percentage of online shopping occurs between 9 PM and 2 AM. When a shopper has a critical sizing or shipping question late at night and there is no live support available, they don't wait for your team to open at 9 AM—they abandon their cart and buy elsewhere.

Why Enterprise Tools Like Gorgias & Zendesk Are Overkill

Platforms like Gorgias, Zendesk, and Intercom charge high monthly per-agent fees ($50 to $300/month). For boutique e-commerce brands, custom jewelry shops, and direct-to-consumer stores, this recurring overhead eats into profit margins unnecessarily.

  • Seamlessly fits into your Shopify theme with custom brand colors, font matching, and custom avatars.
