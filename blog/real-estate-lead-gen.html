The Showing Dilemma

Realtors spend their days on the road: hosting open houses, negotiating inspections, and showing luxury properties. You cannot answer your website live chat while walking a buyer through a home. By the time you get back to your laptop in the evening, that prospective home buyer has already moved on to another listing agent.

How an AI Realtor Assistant Works

Unlike a basic contact form that passive visitors ignore, an AI real estate assistant engages visitors proactively:

  • Automated Showing Booking: Integrates directly with your Calendly or Google Calendar link so qualified buyers can schedule a property showing on your open slots.
