The Hidden Markup of Commercial SaaS Chatbots

What most small business owners and e-commerce entrepreneurs don't realize is that these platforms do not possess proprietary AI. Under the hood, they simply connect to standard foundational AI models, wrap a generic UI around it, and mark up the price by over 5,000%.

"If you cancel your $150/month SaaS chatbot subscription, your bot instantly vanishes. You don't own the code, you don't own the widget, and you've spent thousands of dollars renting a tool that should be an owned company asset."

Furthermore, these tools often impose artificial message caps. The moment your marketing campaign succeeds and website traffic spikes, they hit you with tier upgrades or shut off the assistant entirely until you pay more.
